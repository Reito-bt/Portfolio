Digital Audio Design Assignment 2

Audio mixing, mastering, and collaborative sound production for the project.

What I did

I was responsible for the overall mix and mastering of the audio elements in this assignment. My contribution centered on shaping the sound so it felt balanced, immersive, and aligned with the project's tone.

  • Mixed and mastered the audio in a 5.1 surround sound format for a balanced and immersive result.
  • Used DSP techniques such as equalization, compression, and reverb to improve audio quality.
  • Worked with team members to record and track audio assets like footsteps and ambient sounds.
  • Tested and refined the audio based on feedback and project requirements.
