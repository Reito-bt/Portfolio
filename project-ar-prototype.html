Unity-based AR prototyping with interactive elements and iterative refinement.

What I did

I was responsible for developing and implementing the Unity-based AR prototype for this assignment. The focus was on creating an experience that felt interactive while still being practical to test and refine during development.

  • Designed and developed an AR prototype in Unity to showcase interactive and immersive elements.
  • Implemented AR features such as object tracking and user interactions.
  • Collaborated with team members to integrate the prototype into the overall project in a cohesive way.
  • Used Unity's AR Foundation framework to improve performance and visual quality.
  • Tested and iterated on the prototype based on feedback and project requirements.
